# Attendance Calculator for Students

A free, easy-to-use online attendance calculator designed specifically for college students to track their attendance percentage, plan leaves, and maintain the required 75% attendance.

## Features

- **Calculate Attendance Percentage**: Instantly calculate your current attendance based on working days and absences
- **Semester-wise Tracking**: Support for both odd (July-December) and even (January-June) semesters
- **Holiday Management**: Built-in Indian government holidays with customizable selection
- **Smart Recommendations**: Get personalized advice on how many days you can miss or need to attend
- **Leave Planning**: Plan your leaves while maintaining minimum attendance requirements
- **Mobile Responsive**: Works perfectly on all devices

## How to Use

1. **Select Academic Year**: Choose your current academic year
2. **Select Semester**: Pick odd or even semester
3. **Select Months**: Click on the months you attended college
4. **Select Holidays**: Check the holidays declared by your college
5. **Enter Details**: Input number of Saturdays with college and total absent days
6. **Calculate**: Click the calculate button to see your attendance percentage
